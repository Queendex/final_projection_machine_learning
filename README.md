# final_project_on_machine_learning_using_the_mall_dataset
Tackles supervised and unsuoervised machine learning using mall dataset. the customers for the organization were segmented according to their spending pattern using unsupervised machine learning.
This segmentation analysis identifies key customer groups based on income, spending habits, age, and loyalty.
The goal is to understand customer diversity, spending behavior, and develop targeted marketing strategies.
Supervised learning were used to make the following predictions:
Predicting Spending Score: after running some predictions we found out that Predicting a customer's spending propensity as a continuous value.
Predictive Objective: Predict a customer's Spending Score based on their demographic and economic profile.
The model struggles with high spenders and some low spenders are mixed up(the model confuses them for medium spenders)
The following problems that could have caused this inadequacies are: the model fail to understand why customers spend differently. The dataset lacks some factors that can be strong predictive such as(behavioral features) frequency of purchase, customer loyalty points, product categories bought,(demographic factors)family size, occupation, location etc.

Predicting Spending Category: Customers were labelled/classified as Low, Medium and High Spenders based on their Age, Gender and Income.
This action taken helps the sales teams to recognize customer type and avoid treating all customers the same.
The Medium Spender had the highest percentage for this prediction and we can be able to predict these categories based on Age(which did not have so much influence on the spending score),Gender(which also did not affect or influence the spending score), Annual Income(which was a strong predictive factor to influence spending score).
It is seen that the higher the annual income, the possibility of having more high spending score.
 
 INITIAL INSIGHTS:  Medium spenders form the largest group (balanced spending behavior)  
High spenders show varied income levels (not just high-income customers)
• Age distribution varies across spending categories
• Gender distribution is relatively balanced across categories

Some reccommendations based on our analysis are as follow :Launch targeted email campaigns for each categories of spenders
Train your staffs on how to approach customers that falls in their various classification. For example, high spender customer should be greeted warmly, shown luxury and expensive items first before suggesting on add-ons and upgrades, medium spender customer should be shown good quality items  on a fair price and suggest bundles or sets, low spenders customer should be greeted respectfully, show them value items on a great price deals and avoid pushing them with expensive items.
Items in the store should be properly arranged to suit all the categories of spenders that comes into the mall.
Apply the money rule which states on spending marketing money where you make your money(high spenders and medium spenders)
